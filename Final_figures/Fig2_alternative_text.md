Figure 2 Alt Text - Seven panels display how spike frequency adaptation (SFA) and short-term synaptic depression (STD) stabilize neural networks. Panel A shows a sparse synaptic weight matrix, and B shows its weight distribution histogram. Panel C displays unstable eigenvalue outliers for this baseline network. Panel D contrasts time-series activity, demonstrating an SFA-only network becomes chaotic during stimulation, while a network with combined SFA and STD remains near the edge of chaos. Panel E reveals that combined adaptation dynamically modulates the effective connectivity matrix, and panel F shows this pulls eigenvalues toward stability. Panel G provides a statistical comparison of stability during stimulation versus no stimulation, confirming that combining SFA and STD maintains consistent network stability.
