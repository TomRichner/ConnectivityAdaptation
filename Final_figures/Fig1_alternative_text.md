Figure 1 Alt Text - Six scatter plots on the complex plane display how various synaptic connectivity rules impact network eigenvalues and stability. Subpanel A shows a slightly positive mean weight creating an unstable outlier. Subpanel B demonstrates that negative feedback globally stabilizes all eigenvalues. Subpanel C reveals that applying Dale's law introduces unstable outliers. Subpanel D illustrates that perfectly balancing excitatory and inhibitory inputs restabilizes the network. Subpanel E shows unequal weight distributions concentrating eigenvalues near the center. Finally, Subpanel F demonstrates that balancing fails to constrain outliers in sparse networks, highlighting the necessity for dynamic adaptation.
